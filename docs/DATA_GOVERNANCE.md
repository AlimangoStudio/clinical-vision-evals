# Data governance and source provenance

Only entirely invented, nonidentifiable demonstration records, plus an original geometric illustration, are distributed in this repository. These examples are not clinical observations or a calibrated diagnostic reference set.

- Every record contains source title, original public URL, research limitations, license, and unvalidated status.
- Referenced educational pages are linked, not scraped or republished.
- Contributors must not submit patient records, identifiable photographs, hospital reports, real pathology images, private medical data, secrets, or sensitive metadata.
- Future real clinical datasets require documented consent or lawful basis, source licensing, a clinical governance board, access control, independent privacy review, and a separate non-public storage boundary.
- Public issues and PR descriptions must not include patient information.
- Do not publish data copied from PAPPS.
