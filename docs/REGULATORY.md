# Regulatory considerations

This project is not intended or validated for diagnosis, screening, triage, cancer detection or treatment. The sample matcher is a synthetic research tool and outputs no calibrated probabilities.

A disclaimer alone does not remove medical-device obligations if a future product is designed to interpret images or recommend specific tests. Review applicable requirements, including Philippine FDA software-as-medical-device and Data Privacy Act considerations, with qualified counsel/regulatory specialists before deployment. Investigate other jurisdictions separately where deployed.

Reference: [US FDA clinical decision support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/step-6-software-function-intended-provide-clinical-decision-support).
