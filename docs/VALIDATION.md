# Clinical validation roadmap

**Current status: synthetic-only research prototype, not medically validated.**

Before any clinical application: define intended use with clinicians; identify acceptable data provenance and image rights; obtain review-board/ethics input where applicable; construct a representative adjudicated dataset and clear reference standard; pre-register blinded evaluation; quantify false negatives, false positives, calibration, prevalence sensitivity and subgroup performance; test human-factor effects; assess clinical security, privacy, operational drift and fallback workflows.

For serious conditions such as skin cancer, avoid relying on model similarity to reassure a clinician or patient that a lesion is harmless. No unchecked automatic disease exclusion. Escalation and testing remain clinician-owned.

Publish measured results and known limitations, not marketing claims. Regulatory clearance may be required even when a physician reviews outputs.
